Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly am I buying?

A digital cross stitch pattern collection in PDF format. It includes printable charts, color keys, stitch counts, size details and guides. Fabric, thread, needles and finished items are not included.

How are the 1,000 patterns counted?

The Complete Collection contains 1,000 individual artworks, with 100 designs in each of ten themes. Each has a complete subject and its own chart grid and thread key. Color and monochrome versions, paper formats, recolors and mirrored copies do not count as extra designs.

Can I use these as a beginner?

The patterns use full cross stitches only, without fractional stitches or backstitch. Shaded scenes vary in detail, stitch count and number of colors; start with a simpler design if you are new. Basic chart instructions are included with the pattern files. The Complete Collection also includes the expanded Visual Beginner Guide, with illustrated steps and a practice flower.

What is the difference between A4 and US Letter?

US Letter is the paper size commonly used in the United States (8.5 × 11 inches). A4 measures 210 × 297 mm. Both versions contain the same patterns; choose the PDF that matches your paper.

Do I need to print the entire PDF?

No. Use the clickable visual index to choose one project. Print its overview and either its color or monochrome tiles. A detailed chart can span up to four tiles; each shows its position and global row and column numbers. You do not need to print both versions.

What thread and fabric should I use?

Each design has its own suggested DMC thread palette, symbols and stitch counts. Start with 14-count Aida and two strands of cotton, adjusting to your fabric and preferred coverage. Each overview also lists design-area sizes for 16- and 18-count Aida. Screen and printed swatches are approximate; check actual floss.

Are the finished-project pictures photographs?

The hoop and framed-project images are AI-generated visualizations based on the charts. They show possible finished looks and may differ in small details. The designs have been checked digitally, but have not been physically test-stitched. The pattern-sheet previews show actual PDF pages.

Do the two collections overlap?

Yes. Every Essential design is also included in the Complete Collection. The Complete Collection adds 500 designs, a project tracker, 12 gift tags, and three exclusive visual bonus guides: Beginner, Finishing, and Color & Fabric. Choose the single collection that suits you.

Can I give my finished stitching as a gift?

Yes. The collection is for personal use, and you can give your finished stitching as gifts. Do not share, resell or redistribute the digital charts or artwork.

Is this a subscription?

No. The displayed price is a one-time payment for the selected collection. Future updates or additional collections are not part of this offer.
